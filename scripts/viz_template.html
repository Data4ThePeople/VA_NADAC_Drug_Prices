VA: Big4 prices in effect November 1, 2026, from the VA National Acquisition Center pharmaceutical price list. Big4 is the most the VA can be charged; it can pay less. Medicare: median 30-day unit cost across Part D plans, CMS Quarterly Prescription Drug Plan pricing file, 2026 Q3. Part D prices are before most manufacturer rebates, which are not public. Each bar is the median across the drug's packages. Drugs: top 100 brands by 2024 Part D claims (CMS Medicare Part D Spending by Drug); spending is 2024 Part D gross spending from the same file, before rebates. 10 drugs are not shown because they have no Big4 price or no Part D price. Negotiated drugs: CMS, 2026 negotiated prices. Built by Data 4 The People.
