Plans and tiers: CMS Quarterly Prescription Drug Plan formulary, plan information and beneficiary cost files, 2026 Q3 (released October 2026). Plans are contract-plan-segment combinations on five- or six-tier drug lists, counted as plans, not enrollees. CMS lists each covered drug product once per plan with one example NDC. A product counts as generic when that NDC is approved under an abbreviated new drug application (ANDA) in the FDA NDC Directory; brand-name drugs are NDA or BLA products. Tier names follow CMS's standard layout; tier 6, where used, holds low-cost generics. First generic approval: the earliest original ANDA approval for the same active ingredient and dosage form in Drugs@FDA. Cost sharing: initial coverage phase, one-month supply at a retail pharmacy.
