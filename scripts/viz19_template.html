Rebates and other payments to Part D plans, as a share of drug costs, 2016 to 2026

Payments that Medicare Part D plans collect after a prescription is filled, mostly rebates from drug makers, as a share of total Part D drug costs. Before 2024 they also included fees pharmacies paid back to plans. All drugs, generic and brand. 2025 and 2026 are estimates. Hover or tap the line for each year.

Source: 2026 Annual Report of the Boards of Trustees of the Federal Hospital Insurance and Federal Supplementary Medical Insurance Trust Funds, Table IV.B8, "Direct and indirect remuneration," expressed as a percentage of total drug costs; 2016 to 2024 historical, 2025 and 2026 intermediate estimates. In 2024 a CMS rule moved pharmacy fees into the price at the pharmacy counter, which removed them from this measure. The Trustees expect the 2026 negotiated prices to reduce rebates. Averages across all drugs; generic drugs carry little or no rebate, so the share for brand-name drugs is higher. Built by Data 4 The People.
